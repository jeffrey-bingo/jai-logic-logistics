Privacy Policy

Brand: JAI Logic Logistics (Jai Logic Logistics) · Effective Date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JAI Logic Logistics (Jai Logic Logistics) collects, uses, and protects information provided by business customers and receiving contacts in connection with our B2B supply verification and delivery logistics services.

1. Information We Collect

When you coordinate a delivery or optionally enroll in SMS delivery notifications, we may collect the following business and contact information:

  • Business Name — the commercial or institutional entity receiving the shipment.
  • Contact Name — the authorized receiving or scheduling contact person.
  • Delivery Address — the physical destination, dock, or receiving location for the shipment.
  • Mobile Phone Number — the phone number designated to receive SMS delivery updates.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for transactional SMS delivery ETA notifications and scheduled delivery coordination, including confirming dispatch status, providing estimated arrival windows, and resolving receiving or dock-access logistics.

3. SMS Data Protection & Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information is shared only with service providers strictly necessary to deliver our transactional SMS notifications and fulfill scheduled shipments.

4. Opt-Out & Contact Information

You may opt out of SMS delivery notifications at any time by replying STOP to any message. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us at jai.logic.tech@gmail.com.
